[OK] GET|POST /agents/customers_paginated/:column_id      *** PREFERRED READ ***
       -> One call returns ALL customers' values for a column.
          [{customer_id, domain, "<Column Name>": "...", section_id,
            column_name, value_structured:{string_value}}]
          Read value_structured.string_value (stable key).

[OK] GET  /research/clever_column/:column_id/cell/:entity_id
       (entity_id = customer_id) single-cell read; values byte-identical to bulk.
       -> {column_id, entity_id, cell_value, output:{type,text,sources}, updated_at}
       WARNING: `output` is a DICT and therefore truthy. A naive "first non-empty

[OK] POST /research/clever_column/:column_id/refresh_by_tab/:tab_id   *** THE TRIGGER ***
       Empty body. Pass rox_org_id as tab_id to refresh ALL accounts.
       Verified on Opportunity Signal: enqueued 1x CUSTOM_COLUMN_GENERATION +
       21x CUSTOM_CELL_GENERATION within 20s, all COMPLETED in ~175s, with
       genuinely new content (Genpact 7->2, HP 4->8).
       WARNING: returns "Successfully triggered refresh" for ANY uuid, including
                all-zeros, and for columns where nothing happens. The message is
                NOT validation - confirm real work via /priority_jobs.
       ONLY WORKS ON UI-CREATED COLUMNS (see below).

PARSING GOTCHAS:
  - Score scale is 0-10, NOT 0-100. A naive parse gives 7 and fails a 0-100
    threshold of 60. Multiply by 10.
  - Separators differ: Opportunity Signal uses em-dash (—), Risk Signal hyphen (-).
  - Risk Signal mixes Low|Medium|High with 0-10, and LOW IS GOOD - do not add
    it to a score as-is.
  - Stored values are capped ~300 chars with a trailing "..." (Rox-side, present
    in both bulk and single-cell reads).
